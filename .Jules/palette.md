## 2024-05-13 - [Added ARIA Labels to Sidebar UI]\n**Learning:** This application relies heavily on icon-only buttons in its sidebar templates and dynamic JS creation (e.g. `domManager.js` restore button). These lacked proper explicit `aria-label` attributes for screen readers, meaning users depending on assistive technology had minimal context for actions like closing tabs or deleting spaces.\n**Action:** Add clear, explicit `aria-label` attributes to all custom icon-only actions and dynamically generated tab/space management controls.
